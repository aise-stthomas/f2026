# Week 1 — Design Studio
## "Design a system that flags fraudulent transactions."

**Scope only.** Step 1 of the framework — scope and requirements — and the first half of
step 2, framing the AI task. No boxes. No models. No architecture. 

**Format.** Build in pairs — sketch it on one page, argue, merge into one sketch. Then
we review as a group. The answers document is handed out after.

**What a step-1 sketch contains.** Who the users are — all of them. What decision is
actually being made. The three numbers: scale, latency budget, cost budget. What "good"
means, and for whom. What happens when the system is wrong.

**Then: the envelope.** Write your sketch up in a document — one page, unpolished —
and save it somewhere you will find it again. Don't reread it. In Week 13 you'll open it
and redesign it with everything the course gave you. The gap between the two answers is
the course.
